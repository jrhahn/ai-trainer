- "plan_query": asking about their training plan, schedule, or specific workouts
- "workout_modification": requesting a change, swap, or skip of a workout
- "performance_question": asking about their performance, FTP, progress, or race results
- "science_question": asking about training physiology, nutrition, recovery science, or methodology
- "general_coaching": general coaching advice, motivation, or strategy
Return ONLY a valid JSON object with these fields:
- "category": one of the categories above
- "needs_science_rag": true when the question would benefit from cycling science research (science_question or a detailed physiology/methodology question); false for simple plan queries, workout swaps, and schedule questions
