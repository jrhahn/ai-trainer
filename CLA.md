## Points for the legal review (remove before this goes into force)

1. **Exclusive rights under German law.** Copyright itself cannot be
   transferred (§ 29 UrhG), only rights of use. Section 2 grants an exclusive
   right of use. Is that enough for the Maintainer to relicense and to enforce
   against third parties? Is the licence back in section 3 needed or helpful?
2. **Unknown types of use.** § 31a UrhG requires written form for granting
   rights in types of use unknown at the time of the contract. Does a commit
   sign-off satisfy that? Would a separate signature be needed, for example a
   form, e-signature or CLA bot?
3. **No payment.** § 32 UrhG gives authors a claim to equitable remuneration,
   and § 32a gives a further claim when a work turns out disproportionately
   profitable. Can section 4 exclude these? Does the open-source exception
   (§ 32 (3) sentence 3, § 32a (3) sentence 3) apply to an exclusive grant to
   one person? If it cannot be excluded, what wording keeps the exposure small?
4. **Contributors outside Germany.** Does the choice of law hold for them?
5. **Employer rights.** Is section 5 enough, or should employed contributors
   supply an employer waiver?
6. **Language.** Should there be a German version, and which one prevails?
